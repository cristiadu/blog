I don't know how to rap
don't know which words 
can fit in my map
don't know which rhyme
will sound corny at last.
I am no Lupe.

I don't know how to sing.
Want to scream from the top of my lungs
but I can't
It's all a blur in my end
It's all my mind looking cement
expecting something concrete.

Here am I the leader?
A fool with some power is so easy to trick;
to sell empty promises.
This isn't your deck of all aces;
that isn't the ice on your water.
Got to try saving your faces.
