They said everyone suffers bullying,
"caring is what causes it!".
Pleading them bullies for some humanity;
suffocating is not the answer.
It's a choice,
you made a choice.

--

Stop! Stop the crying alone
just make yourself at home.
After all it's just a joke;
they are just children
they can't really hurt you.
Sticks and stones may break my bones,
but words will break my soul.

--

They said it is just progress
"Swimming against the current is pointless".
Pleading friends and family for some humanity,
just like I wanted to get as a kid, years ago.
But... suffocating is not the answer, they also say.
It's my choice to use the technology I hate.
You don't have a choice,
speed, money pursuit was the choice.
They made it for you.

-- 

Stop! Stop the crying alone..
Keep all these depressing thoughts at home,
after all it's just progress, evolution.
Tasteless, soulless progress.
These are only machines, only tools.
Complaining just makes you the fool.
They can't really fire you.

--

Sticks and chipsets may break my computerized mind
But words? No, there are only prompts!
This is progress.
The future is so bright, they said.
All of my bullies said.
